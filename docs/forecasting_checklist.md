# Preparing for the event: how a forecaster could have seen the May snow coming

This page walks through the signals, day by day, that point to snow on the İzmir mountains on 3 May 2026,
and checks them against what actually happened.

## Timeline of signals

| When | Signal | Where to look | What it meant |
|---|---|---|---|
| 28–29 Apr | İzmir 24–26 °C, dry | TSMS forecasts | Warm start – a strong contrast is coming |
| 30 Apr | TSMS: cold, wet air mass from the Balkans for ~5 days | TSMS / news (NTV) | Pattern change announced |
| 1 May | 500 hPa trough NW Russia → Black Sea, −30 °C core | 500 hPa chart | Cold air aloft heading south |
| 2 May | Closed low forms (≈547 gpdm); northerly 50–70 km/h over the Aegean | 500 hPa, surface | Cold advection begins |
| 2 May | Yellow code, 37 provinces; "snow mixed with rain on İzmir heights" | TSMS warning | Snow explicitly forecast for high ground |
| 3 May | Cut-off low ≈544 gpdm, T850 ≈ 0 °C, convective cells | 500/850 hPa, satellite | Heavy showers + snow level near 1,100 m |
| 3 May midday | Snow in Gölcük / Bozdağ (~1,100 m) | News (Haber7, AA) | **Event** |
| 4 May | Low moves to the Black Sea, Aegean clears | Charts, satellite | Cold spell ends gradually |

## Snow checklist (rules of thumb)

| Check | Threshold for snow | 3 May 2026 | ✔ / ✘ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Precipitation expected? | yes, preferably heavy | 13.6–16.5 mm (ERA5), heavy showers | ✔ |
| T850 | ≤ 0 °C for hills, ≤ −5 °C for low ground | ≈ 0 °C | ✔ for hills only |
| 500 hPa cold pool | ≤ −25 °C in spring | −25 to −30 °C | ✔ |
| 1000–500 hPa thickness | < 540 dam near sea level | > 540 dam at the coast | ✘ coast → rain |
| Wet-bulb zero level | within ~300 m of the ground | estimated a few hundred m above 1,100 m | ✔ borderline |
| Surface T at the site | ≤ +1…+2 °C | ≈ 3–5 °C (ERA5 reduced to 1,100 m) | borderline – cooling in showers needed |

**Verdict:** snow was likely on the mountains (≥ ~1,100 m) and unlikely at the coast. This is exactly what happened.

## Why the snow line was lower than the "dry" estimate
1. With 6.5 K/km, the ski-centre forecast (0–2 °C at ~1,540 m) gives ≈3–5 °C at 1,100 m – too warm for snow in dry air.
2. In heavy showers, snowflakes **melt and evaporate**, taking latent heat from the air; the lowest layer cools towards 0 °C.
3. The snow level can drop several hundred metres below the freezing level – here to about 1,100 m.

## Lessons
- **Read the forecast's elevation.** TSMS "Bozdağ 0–2 °C" was for the ski centre, not the villages.
- **Reanalysis needs terrain correction.** ERA5 placed Bozdağ at 685 m and produced no snow; corrected to 1,540 m it matched the forecast.
- **Convective cooling matters in spring.** Cut-off lows with cold cores can bring snow below the "expected" snow line.
- **Warning counts change during the day.** 37 → 41 → 43 provinces between issues on 2–3 May; quote the issue time.
